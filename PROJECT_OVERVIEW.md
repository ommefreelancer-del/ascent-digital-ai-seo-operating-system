# Ascent Digital AI SEO Operating System (ADASOS)

## Project Overview

The Ascent Digital AI SEO Operating System (ADASOS) is a modular, production-oriented AI operating system designed to assist with ethical, data-driven SEO and digital marketing workflows.

The system coordinates specialized AI agents through a centralized Boss Agent to automate repetitive work, improve consistency, and support informed decision-making while keeping humans in control of all high-impact actions.

---

# Vision

Build a scalable AI operating system that helps manage SEO campaigns, content production, website optimization, outreach, reporting, and business operations through coordinated AI agents.

The system is intended to improve productivity, maintain quality, reduce manual work, and support long-term business growth.

---

# Core Objectives

- Improve SEO workflow efficiency.
- Standardize agency processes.
- Support data-driven decision making.
- Reduce repetitive manual work.
- Maintain high quality and consistency.
- Keep humans in control of important decisions.
- Follow ethical AI and SEO best practices.

---

# System Architecture

The operating system follows a multi-agent architecture coordinated by the Boss Agent.

Major functional areas include:

- SEO Strategy
- Keyword Research
- Competitor Intelligence
- Content Strategy
- Content Writing
- On-Page SEO
- Technical SEO
- Website Auditing
- Off-Page SEO
- Outreach & Negotiation
- CRM Management
- Google Sheets Integration
- Website Development
- Website Management
- Graphic Design
- Google Business Profile
- Client Reporting
- Business Development
- Performance Analytics
- Administrative Operations

Each agent has clearly defined responsibilities and communicates through the official Agent Communication Protocol.

---

# Human-in-the-Loop

ADASOS is designed as an AI-assisted operating system, not a fully autonomous system.

Human approval is required before performing high-impact actions such as:

- Sending emails
- Negotiating pricing
- Publishing content
- Updating external systems
- Deploying production changes
- Deleting records
