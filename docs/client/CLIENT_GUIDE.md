# Client Guide

Welcome. This is the starting point for using the SEO platform (Ascent Digital AI SEO Operating System, delivered by SM Digital) as a client or day-to-day user. It's a chat-based workspace backed by 27 specialist AI assistants who each focus on a different part of SEO — audits, keyword research, content writing, technical fixes, reporting, and more. You don't need to know their names or pick one yourself; just describe what you need in plain language, and the system routes your request to the right specialist automatically.

## What to read, depending on what you need

- **New here?** Start with `client/GETTING_STARTED.md`.
- **Want to know what the system can actually do?** Read `client/FEATURES_AND_CAPABILITIES.md`.
- **Curious about the specialists you're chatting with?** Read `client/AGENT_GUIDE.md`.
- **Wondering what happens step by step when you ask for something big (like "write me an article" or "audit my site")?** Read `client/WORKFLOW_GUIDE.md`.
- **Asked to approve something and not sure what that means?** Read `client/APPROVALS_AND_HUMAN_CONTROL.md`.
- **Want to know exactly what to provide and what you'll get back?** Read `client/INPUTS_AND_OUTPUTS.md`.
- **Something not working right?** Read `client/TROUBLESHOOTING.md`.
- **Quick question?** Check `client/FAQ.md` first.

## The two things worth understanding before you start

1. **The system never makes a real, permanent change to your live website, repository, or connected accounts without asking you to approve it first.** This is a built-in safeguard, not just a policy — see `client/APPROVALS_AND_HUMAN_CONTROL.md`.
2. **Not every reply reflects a live lookup.** Some answers are grounded in real, freshly gathered data (a real crawl of your site, your real search performance numbers). Others are the assistant's own well-informed writing on a topic, without a live tool behind that specific answer. The system is built to never claim it did something it didn't — if you're ever unsure whether a specific claim reflects a real action taken on your behalf, just ask, or see `client/AGENT_GUIDE.md`.

## Getting help

If something seems wrong or a reply doesn't make sense, check `client/TROUBLESHOOTING.md` first, or contact your account administrator.
