# Agent Guide

You're chatting with one system, but behind the scenes it's made up of 27 specialists, each focused on a different part of SEO. You never need to pick one by name — just describe what you need — but this page explains what each one focuses on, in case you're curious or want to ask for a specific capability directly.

Each entry notes whether that specialist typically gives you **real, data-backed results** or **expert guidance** (see `client/FEATURES_AND_CAPABILITIES.md` for what that distinction means). A few specialists do both, depending on exactly what you ask.

## Website health & technical SEO

- **Website Audit** — runs a real, comprehensive health check on any URL. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Technical SEO** — focuses specifically on crawlability, site speed, and technical health issues. *Real, data-backed.*
- **On-Page SEO** — reviews and recommends on-page optimization (titles, headers, content structure). *Mostly expert guidance; real when reached through the automatic audit process.*
- **Performance & Analytics** — reports on real ranking, traffic, and Core Web Vitals data from your connected accounts. *Real, data-backed when an account is connected.*

## Content

- **Keyword Research & Search Intent** — real keyword and search-intent data. *Real, data-backed.*
- **SEO Strategy** — recommends an overall strategic direction. *Expert guidance.*
- **Content Strategy** — plans what content to create and why. *Real, as part of the automatic content pipeline.*
- **SEO Content (Writing)** — writes the actual articles, pages, and meta content, with images and a quality check. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Guest Posting & Digital PR** — drafts guest-post-style content and outreach angles. *Expert guidance.*

## Website changes & management

- **Web Development** — proposes and, only with your explicit approval, applies a real code change to your connected repository. *Real, and approval-gated.*
- **Website Management** — helps plan and describe website/CMS content changes. *Expert guidance in chat; real WordPress publishing is available separately through Settings → Integrations.*

## Research & intelligence

- **Competitor Intelligence** — gathers real information about a competitor's site. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Contact Intelligence** — helps identify relevant contacts for outreach. *Expert guidance.*
- **Prospecting** — finds real, evidence-based website opportunities for a campaign. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Publisher Qualification** — evaluates whether a prospect meets your campaign's requirements. *Expert guidance.*

## Outreach & relationships

- **Outreach** — drafts outreach messages. *Expert guidance.*
- **Reply & Negotiation** — helps manage publisher conversations and pricing discussions; never sends anything or agrees to a price without your approval. *Expert guidance.*
- **Business Development** — general business-growth advice. *Expert guidance.*
- **Client Relationship Management** — helps manage client relationship notes and follow-ups. *Expert guidance.*
- **AI CRM** — conversational CRM-style assistance. *Expert guidance.*

## Reporting & tracking

- **Client Reporting** — generates client-facing performance reports. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Campaign Tracking** — tracks the real, ongoing status of a named campaign. *Real, data-backed.*

## Google & design tools

- **Google Business Profile** — helps with your Business Profile presence. *Expert guidance in chat; a real connection is available separately through Settings → Integrations.*
- **Google Sheets Integration** — reports on your connection status and can perform a real, approval-gated spreadsheet cleanup. *Real, data-backed.*
- **Graphic Design** — searches for real, royalty-free stock images and video for your projects. *Real, data-backed.*

## Governance & administration

- **Admin** — answers questions about system governance and access using real, current account data. *Real for governance questions; expert guidance for broader documentation/process topics.*

## A note on honesty

Every specialist here is built to never claim it took an action it didn't actually take. If a reply describes something like "I've published this" or "I've sent that email" that you didn't separately see and approve, treat it as a description of what *would* happen next, not a confirmed action — and feel free to ask directly, or check with your administrator.
